The chosen design (owner, 2026-11-17): one section per part of the computer, with a Board view that draws the slots and connectors. It is a Resolute tool window, so the sidebar, content header, status bar, Activity, Settings and About are the suite's. The accent follows Windows; Amber, the suite's fallback, is shown. The machine shown is example data. design/SPEC.md is the contract for building it 1:1.
